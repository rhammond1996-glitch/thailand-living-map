# Destination photography: sourcing audit

**Audit date:** 22 November 2026. **Scope:** all saved Bangkok and Chiang Mai map markers, including the Chiang Mai itinerary.

## Editorial rules

1. A link to a store does **not** establish the identity of a photograph. Require an identifiable original photo page and a match to that venue, brand, collection, landmark, or explicitly identified event.
2. Prefer the exact storefront or interior; brand products can illustrate a fashion/fragrance stop but must be labeled as collection photography, **not** a storefront.
3. Broad categories (markets, creative streets) can have representative scenes only when sourced from that named district or venue.
4. Wikimedia Commons and Wikipedia are acceptable for documented landmarks and heritage sites. Avoid matching single, ambiguous brand-name keywords.
5. If no unambiguous photo is found, keep the designed fallback poster and link to visitor photos. **Never substitute a vaguely similar place.**
6. A sourced image is not necessarily licensed for republication. Original publishers and images may retain copyright; **permission and hotlinking rights have not been established for all externally hosted photographs**. Source pages and credits are provided for editorial verification, not as proof of a reuse license.
7. Broken image links are removed from the on-screen card in favor of another selected photo or the graphic fallback.
