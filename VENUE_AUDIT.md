# Thailand Living Map — Venue & Photo Audit

**Audit date:** 22 November 2026. **Coverage:** 52 live-sheet locations + 24 Chiang Mai itinerary stops = 76 unique destination IDs.

This is the internal working ledger behind the destination cards. **Verified links** are supported by official business sites, venue pages or clearly attributed editorial directories. **Existence and address are different questions:** a brand can be genuine while its saved map coordinate still needs a fresh check. We have not purchased image licenses or assumed public social photographs can be re-hosted.

**Photo policy:** favor the exact venue's Wikipedia article lead photo, a Commons category named for the actual place, or images whose filenames explicitly match the venue. Up to 14 candidates can be shown when available. Do not fill gaps with unrelated neighborhood images. Use official website/Instagram and visitor-photo links for places without attributable images.

**Review priority:**

- **Vintage Wings, Chiang Mai**: no sufficiently credible current storefront source was confirmed. Retained as a candidate, clearly flagged for review.
- **Carnival, Matter Makers, Gongdid, Metal Studio**: brands/studios exist, but verify the saved branch or exact entrance using the linked directory before traveling.
- **GALAXYCNX, PALIT, TRES, Garçon Dept., Loft Eyes, PROMTHONG**: business sources located. Confirm the exact store location if plans depend on it.
- **Bo Sang Umbrella Festival**: January 2027 dates are still unconfirmed by the organizer; do not represent January 15 as a guaranteed opening date.

## Follow-up workflow

1. For any source-screened entry that is uncertain, open the official page and confirm it is the same venue and not a similarly named place.
2. If the user rejects a saved shop or it no longer operates, remove it from the master Sheet (or itinerary dataset), not merely from a UI list. No entries were removed automatically in this audit.
3. For photos that cannot be properly associated with the exact venue, keep the official or visitor-photo link rather than showing an attractive but incorrect image.
4. Verify opening hours, event dates, advance bookings and entry restrictions closer to the January 2027 trip.
